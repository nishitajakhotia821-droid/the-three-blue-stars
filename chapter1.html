CLASS VI

Where Everything Began...

The Classroom

Every unforgettable story begins somewhere. Ours didn't begin in a famous place or during an extraordinary event. It began inside an ordinary classroom, where three students had no idea that they were about to become lifelong friends.

We laughed over silly things, helped each other with schoolwork, and slowly created memories that would stay with us long after school ended.

The First Conversations

Maybe it all started with a simple "Hi." Maybe it was because we sat near each other. Maybe it was a homework question. None of us remembers the exact moment.

But somehow, one small conversation became hundreds of conversations, and those conversations became years of friendship.

School Memories

School gave us more than books and exams. It gave us inside jokes. It gave us unforgettable lunches. It gave us celebrations, competitions, classroom fun, and memories that still make us smile today.

Looking back now, those ordinary school days became some of the most extraordinary moments of our lives.

"Every friendship has a beginning. Ours just happened to begin in one classroom."
