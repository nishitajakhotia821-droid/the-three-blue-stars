CHAPTER 01

CLASS VI

Where Everything Began

Every unforgettable story begins somewhere. Ours didn't begin with something extraordinary. It began inside one ordinary classroom, where three students slowly became lifelong friends.

We shared notebooks. We laughed over silly jokes. We celebrated festivals. We helped each other during exams. We made memories without realizing they would become the best days of our lives.

Looking back now... those ordinary school days became the most extraordinary chapter of our story.

"Some people enter our lives for a season... You two became my home."

Our story has only just begun...

Every chapter ahead holds another memory, another laugh, another promise, and another reminder that true friendship never fades with time.
