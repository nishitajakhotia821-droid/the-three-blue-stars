CHAPTER 05

THE FUTURE ARC

No Matter Where Life Takes Us...

2026

Today

Life has become busy. College, work, responsibilities, and countless new experiences keep us occupied. Yet every conversation between us still feels exactly like our school days never ended.

2028

Chasing Dreams

We'll be building careers, learning new skills, meeting new people, and creating new stories. But no achievement will ever replace the friends who stood beside us from the beginning.

2032

Different Places, Same Bond

Maybe one of us will live in another city. Maybe another will move abroad. Time zones may change. Addresses may change. But friendship doesn't follow maps.

2040

Forever Friends

Years later, we'll probably laugh about the same old jokes, remember our school memories, and wonder how time passed so quickly. Some friendships grow old. Ours simply grows stronger.

Our Promise

No matter how many years pass... No matter how busy life becomes... If one of us says, "I need you." The other two will be there. No explanations. No conditions. No excuses. Just friendship. Always.

"Friendship isn't about growing up together. It's about growing... without growing apart."
