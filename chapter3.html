The Battles Nobody Else Saw

Some Stories Stay Between Friends

Every person fights battles that the world never sees. There are days filled with smiles, and there are days filled with silence. Through every chapter of life, true friendship quietly stands beside us.

We grew together. We laughed together. We worried together. And even during moments when words weren't enough, simply knowing you both were there made everything feel lighter.

Not every memory needs to be shared with the world. Some memories belong only to the people who lived them. That's what makes them special.

A Letter From Me 💙

Dear Govind and Harsh,

Thank you for becoming more than classmates. Thank you for becoming family. School gave us many lessons, but the greatest lesson it gave me was the value of true friendship.

No matter how much time passes, no matter where life takes us, I hope we always find time to laugh together, remember old memories, and create new ones.

If I had the chance to relive my school life, I wouldn't change a single thing, because it gave me both of you.

— Nishita 💙
