SIDE QUESTS

Every Memory Was an Adventure

MISSION 01

Our First Laugh

Every friendship begins with a smile. Maybe it was a silly joke, a funny classroom moment, or something that still makes us laugh today. That first laugh became the beginning of countless happy memories.

MISSION 02

Our First Fight

No friendship is perfect. We disagreed. We argued. But somehow every little fight only made our bond stronger. Looking back now, those moments make us smile.

MISSION 03

The Secret Mission

Real friendship begins when trust is shared. We learned that no matter what happened, we could always count on each other. Some memories are too special to explain— they simply stay in our hearts.

MISSION 04

School Adventures

Every school event became another adventure. Annual Day. Sports Day. Republic Day. Independence Day. They weren't just celebrations... they became chapters in our story.

MISSION 05

Rare Meetings

Life became busy. Different schedules. Different responsibilities. Yet every time we meet, it feels as if we were never apart.

MISSION 06

Still Together

Time changed many things. But it never changed us. Distance never defeated our friendship, because true friendships never need constant conversations.

"Friendship isn't measured by how often we meet. It's measured by how comfortable silence feels."
